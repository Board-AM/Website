## Users

- **Prospective investors** — individuals and advisers deciding whether BoardAM's approach is credible and worth a conversation.
- **Credibility checkers** — people who have already heard of BAM (via a meeting, referral or the fund) and visit to verify it is real, rigorous and run by credible people.
- **Partners / referrers** — advisers and platforms who may send investors and need to understand and explain the approach.

## Product Purpose

boardam.com explains Board Assessment Management's investment method: scoring the leadership (executives and boards) of S&P 500 companies and turning those scores into portfolio decisions. Success is a visitor who understands the method and trusts the team behind it. The primary job is understanding; contact is secondary and must not be pushed.

## Positioning

BAM invests on leadership quality: it reads company filings across the S&P 500, scores executives and boards across defined dimensions into a composite leadership score, and builds portfolios long strong leadership / short weak leadership. Thesis: management decisions drive fundamentals, leadership quality is persistent, and markets underprice it. Approach borrows the analytical rigour of credit-risk frameworks from banking/insurance.

## Product Principles

1. Method first: every page should make the leadership-scoring process clearer, not louder.
2. Rigour must be visible and consistent — numbers, dimensions and scales must agree everywhere.
3. Never imply returns that are not real; illustrative data is labelled as such at the point of display.
4. People are the proof: the team's credentials carry the credibility, so they stay prominent and factual.
5. Restraint over hype: a referred, sceptical visitor should leave convinced by substance, not adjectives.
